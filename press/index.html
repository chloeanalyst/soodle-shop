Soodle press kit

Soodle is a free iPhone step counter with a little sausage dog waiting at home. Walk more and they get happier. Earn hearts, dress them up and make their room cosy.

Get Soodle on the UK App Store

The short version

Every step you take is a walk for your pup. Soodle reads your steps from Apple Health, and your dog's mood follows them all day. Every 1,000 steps earns a heart, and hearts buy jumpers, bandanas, hats, glasses, collars, wallpapers, rugs, beds and more.

Quick facts

Price: free. Platform: iPhone. Available: UK App Store. Ads: none. Account: none needed. Privacy: your steps stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us. Daily goal: 2,500 to 10,000 steps. Extras: home screen and lock screen widgets show your pup in today's mood.
