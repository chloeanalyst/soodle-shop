What Soodle uses

With your permission, Soodle reads your step count from Apple Health. It uses your steps only to show your progress, your pup's mood, hearts and streaks. Your steps, and everything else Soodle saves (your pup, hearts, streaks and settings), stay on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

How long it's kept and deleting it

Soodle keeps this on your iPhone until you delete the app. Deleting Soodle from your iPhone deletes everything it saved. To stop Soodle reading your steps, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Privacy › Apps, and choose Soodle.

This website

soodle.co.uk doesn't use cookies, count visits or load anything from other companies' servers. It uses the fonts already on your device.
