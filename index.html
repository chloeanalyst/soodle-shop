How it works

Three steps to a happy pup

Step 1

Adopt your pup

Meet them at the shelter, give them a name, pick your pup's first collar and sign the adoption papers. They are all yours.

Step 2

Walk together

Pick a daily goal from 2,500 to 10,000 steps. Soodle counts your steps from Apple Health, and your pup’s mood follows them all day.

Step 3

Treat them

Hit your goal and look after them to earn hearts, then spend them on jumpers, beds and cosy things for your pup's room.

A peek inside

Your pup's home, shop and wardrobe

Moods

Your pup's mood follows your steps

A slow day and they'll be waiting with the lead. Nearly there and they're the happiest pup in town. Hit your goal and they nod off for a well-earned nap. Every mood is animated: blinks, sparkles, snoozes and the odd tear.

Tap your pup to give them a pat. A sad pup stops crying and smiles for a moment.

Care

Look after them

Fill your pup's food and water bowls, give them a pat, and after a muddy walk, give them a bath. A sponge, lots of bubbles, a rinse and a sparkle, and they're good as new.

Walk every day to keep a streak going and unlock rewards you can only earn by walking: a photo frame on day 1, a rope toy on day 5 and a sofa bed on day 7.

Wardrobe

Dress them up

Knitted jumpers, bandanas, hats, hair clips, sunglasses, socks, necklaces with your pup's name on the tag, and collars with matching leads. Try anything on in the shop before you spend your hearts.

Then mix and match in the wardrobe, save your favourite looks, or tap Surprise me.

Decorate

A room just for your pup

Wallpapers, rugs, beds, side tables, lamps, wall art and toy baskets, with new pieces added all the time, and a cosy Christmas collection.

Drag things wherever you like, then save your favourite rooms and switch between them.

Widgets

Your pup on your home screen

Check in on your pup all day without opening the app.

Your pup's room. Your pup in its own room, in whatever they are wearing, in today's mood. A trail of paw prints fills in along the bottom as you walk.

Your streak. How many days in a row you have hit your goal, and how many steps are left today.

Your lock screen. A ring that fills as you walk with your pup's little face in the middle, your streak, and a one-line nudge above the time.

To add one: touch and hold your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Soodle.
